Place your vote.

The positive-polls app for your school. Anonymous votes on compliments-only questions, for pupils and students in the UK aged 13 and over. iOS.

  • Every poll is a compliment. Questions come from a fixed list; the one short blank a pupil can fill in is checked before a poll is created.
  • Votes are anonymous to everyone using the app. Real names everywhere else, and no photos anywhere.
  • A date-of-birth and selfie age check before an account exists. Report and block on every poll, story, message and profile.
